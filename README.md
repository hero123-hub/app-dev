# My Favorite Series: Naruto (ナルト)

> *"I'm not gonna run away, I never go back on my word! That's my nindo: my ninja way!"* — **Naruto Uzumaki**

## Overview
**Naruto** is a Japanese shōnen anime series based on Masashi Kishimoto's manga. It follows **Naruto Uzumaki**, a young ninja from the Hidden Leaf Village (*Konohagakure*) who dreams of becoming the **Hokage**—the strongest ninja and leader of his village—while seeking recognition from his peers.
